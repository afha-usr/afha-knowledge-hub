Guiding Principles

The principles that guide AfHA's approach to humanitarian action, coordination, accountability and Africa-led humanitarian response.

Overview The 11 Principles Applying the Principles

Principles at the heart of AfHA

AfHA's humanitarian work is guided by a set of principles intended to promote effective, accountable, inclusive and Africa-led humanitarian action.

These principles provide a common foundation for how humanitarian action is understood, coordinated and delivered, while recognizing the importance of national ownership, complementarity and the needs of vulnerable populations.

AfHA's 11 guiding principles

The principles below provide the foundation for understanding AfHA's approach to humanitarian action.

01

Leadership

Promote strong African leadership in humanitarian action and strengthen the capacity of African institutions to lead humanitarian efforts.

02

Humanity

Place the dignity, protection and well-being of people affected by humanitarian crises at the centre of humanitarian action.

03

Impartiality

Humanitarian action should respond to needs without discrimination and prioritize assistance according to humanitarian need.

04

Independence

Humanitarian action should maintain the independence necessary to address humanitarian needs effectively.

05

Neutrality

Humanitarian action should remain focused on humanitarian needs and not take sides in hostilities or disputes.

06

Solidarity

Promote collective responsibility, cooperation and mutual support in responding to humanitarian crises.

07

Accountability

Promote transparency, responsibility and accountability towards affected populations, institutions and partners.

08

Participation and Ownership

Support meaningful participation and ownership by affected populations, Member States and relevant African institutions.

09

Subsidiarity and Complementarity

Encourage action at the appropriate level while promoting complementarity among national, regional, continental and humanitarian actors.

10

Gender Mainstreaming

Integrate gender considerations throughout humanitarian preparedness, planning, response and recovery efforts.

11

Vulnerable Groups and People with Special Needs

Ensure that humanitarian action recognizes and responds to the specific needs and vulnerabilities of affected populations.

Putting the principles into practice

The guiding principles provide a foundation for humanitarian preparedness, coordination and response. They can also inform the development and application of AfHA's frameworks, guidelines, policies, tools, training and other institutional resources.

The Knowledge Hub brings these resources together to support consistent access to institutional knowledge and to facilitate learning across humanitarian actors.
