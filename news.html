Institutional Structure

A coordinated institutional framework

AfHA's institutional structure is intended to support effective governance, coordination, technical functions and implementation of its humanitarian mandate.

Structure and institutional roles

The Agency's institutional arrangements provide the basis for connecting governance, strategic direction, technical functions and operational support. The structure should enable AfHA to work with African Union institutions, Member States and humanitarian partners while maintaining clear responsibilities and coordination mechanisms.
