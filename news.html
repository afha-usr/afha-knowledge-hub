African Union 9 November 2026

African Union and Uganda Sign Host Agreement for the African Humanitarian Agency (AfHA)

The African Union and the Government of Uganda signed the Host Agreement at State House in Entebbe. The agreement sets out the legal and institutional arrangements for Uganda to host the African Humanitarian Agency.

Curated news and updates from authoritative humanitarian sources.

African Union 11–12 February 2026

AU Executive Council calls for a joint AfHA roadmap

The Council asked the AU Commission and Uganda to develop a joint roadmap and called for faster governing board nominations. See Decision EX.CL/Dec.1324(XLVIII).

Read AU decision (PDF) →
African Union 15–16 February 2025

AU Assembly selects Uganda to host AfHA

The Assembly designated Uganda as host and asked the government and the AU Commission to put the necessary arrangements in place. See Decision Assembly/AU/Dec.909(XXXVIII).

Read AU decision (PDF) →
African Union 18–19 July 2024

AU Executive Council recommends Uganda as host

After reviewing the technical assessment, the Council recommended Uganda to the Assembly as the agency’s host country. See Decision EX.CL/Dec.1269(XLV).
