02 | MANDATE & ROLE

Mandate, Role & Objectives

The mandate, institutional role, objectives and operational requirements that guide the African Humanitarian Agency in strengthening Africa's humanitarian action and response.

Overview Mandate Role Objectives Humanitarian Access Key Areas
01

Overview

AfHA's institutional purpose and strategic orientation.

The African Humanitarian Agency (AfHA) is established to strengthen Africa's capacity to anticipate, prevent and respond to humanitarian crises while promoting a coordinated, effective and Africa-led humanitarian response.

Through its mandate, AfHA works to support Member States, Regional Bodies and Mechanisms and humanitarian actors in addressing the needs of populations affected by crises and emergencies across the continent.
02

Mandate

The core mandate assigned to AfHA.

The mandate of AfHA is to:

  • Streamline the coordination of humanitarian action and response on the continent with Member States.
  • Provide assistance and ensure effective coordination of humanitarian action on the continent.
  • Build and strengthen the capabilities and capacities of AU Member States, Regional Bodies and Mechanisms in preventing, responding, effectively managing, alleviating and contributing to resolving humanitarian crises on the continent.
  • Support a coordinated, effective and Africa-led approach to humanitarian action and response.
03

Role of AfHA

The institutional functions through which AfHA contributes to humanitarian action in Africa.

01

Humanitarian Coordination

Strengthen coordination of humanitarian action and response among Member States, Regional Bodies and Mechanisms and relevant humanitarian actors.

02

Assistance and Response

Provide assistance and contribute to effective and timely humanitarian response to crises and emergencies across the continent.

03

Capacity Strengthening

Build and strengthen the capacities of Member States, Regional Bodies and Mechanisms to prevent, prepare for and respond to humanitarian crises.

04

Prevention and Preparedness

Support efforts to anticipate, prevent and prepare for humanitarian crises and strengthen resilience and response capacities.

05

Humanitarian Policy and Advocacy

Contribute to advancing humanitarian policy, advocacy and strategic dialogue to strengthen Africa's humanitarian architecture.

06

Partnerships and Collaboration

Promote collaboration among the African Union, Member States, Regional Bodies and Mechanisms, humanitarian partners and other relevant stakeholders.

04

Objectives

Strategic objectives supporting AfHA's mandate and institutional role.

01

Strengthen Africa's capacity to anticipate and respond effectively to humanitarian crises.

02

Enhance coordination and coherence of humanitarian action across the continent.

03

Support Member States and Regional Bodies and Mechanisms in developing and strengthening humanitarian capacities.

04

Promote effective, timely and principled humanitarian assistance to populations affected by crises.

05

Strengthen African humanitarian leadership, ownership and institutional capacity.

06

Contribute to improved preparedness, prevention, response and recovery from humanitarian crises.

05

Operational Requirement

Humanitarian access as a requirement for fulfilling AfHA's mandate.

Humanitarian Access

An essential operational requirement for effective humanitarian action and response.

To fulfill its mandate, the AfHA will need to have unimpeded access to humanitarian situations.

Such access is essential for enabling the Agency to understand humanitarian needs, support effective coordination, facilitate timely assistance and contribute to an appropriate response to crises and emergencies across the continent.

Humanitarian access therefore forms an important operational requirement for AfHA to effectively carry out the roles and responsibilities established under its mandate.

06

Key Areas of Action

Areas through which AfHA can operationalize its mandate.

Anticipation & Prevention

Strengthening early warning, preparedness and preventive approaches to humanitarian crises.

Emergency Response

Supporting timely and coordinated humanitarian action in response to emergencies.

Capacity Development

Strengthening national, regional and continental humanitarian capacities.

Coordination

Enhancing collaboration and coordination among humanitarian actors and institutions.

Humanitarian Diplomacy

Supporting dialogue, advocacy and engagement on humanitarian issues affecting the continent.

Knowledge & Learning

Promoting evidence, knowledge exchange, learning and good humanitarian practices.
