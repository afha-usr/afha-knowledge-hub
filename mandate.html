AfHA Institutional Framework

Mandate, Role & Objectives

The mandate, objectives and roles of the African Humanitarian Agency in strengthening humanitarian action, coordination, preparedness and response across the continent.

Mandate Institutional Status Objectives Roles Humanitarian Access
01 | Mandate

Mandate of AfHA

Streamline the coordination of humanitarian action and response on the continent with Member States.

Coordination and Assistance

Provide assistance and ensure effective coordination of humanitarian action on the continent.

Capacity Strengthening

Build and strengthen the capabilities and capacities of AU Member States, Regional Bodies and Mechanisms in preventing, responding to, effectively managing, alleviating and contributing to resolving the humanitarian crises on the continent.

02 | Institutional Status

A Specialized Technical Institution

The AfHA is hereby established as a Specialized Technical Institution of the African Union charged with the responsibility to coordinate and provide humanitarian responses in coordination with affected Member States.

03 | Objectives

Objectives

AfHA's objectives focus on prevention, preparedness, institutional capacity, timely response, adaptation and resilience across continental, regional and national levels.

01

Prevent Humanitarian Crises

Prevent humanitarian crises from unfolding through improved early warning and early action, including anticipatory action, thereby ensuring timely responses to situations that may otherwise develop into humanitarian crises.

02

Enhance Capacity

Enhance the capacity of AU Member States, RECs and AU bodies in cooperation with other agencies to prevent and respond to humanitarian crises.

03

Ensure Timely and Effective Response

Ensure timely and effective responses through establishing and strengthening capacities of continental, regional and national mechanisms.

04

Strengthen Adaptation and Resilience

Together with Member States, design strategies for strengthening adaptation and resilience at the continental, regional and national levels in order to ensure an effective response to humanitarian crises.

04 | Roles

Roles and Functions

The roles of AfHA encompass humanitarian analysis, assistance, coordination, information, research, policy support and access to humanitarian situations.

01

Identify and Map Humanitarian Crises

Identify and map humanitarian crises on the continent.

02

Provide Humanitarian Assistance

Provide humanitarian assistance to AU Member States and persons affected by humanitarian crises.

Regional centres and depots: The AfHA will establish five regional centres to ensure proximity for an effective and timely humanitarian response in each region. These regional centres will also include regional depots to stockpile humanitarian assistance.
03

Strengthen Humanitarian Coordination

Provide strong coordination mechanisms on humanitarian issues, including with the African Standby Force (the Civil-Military element).

04

Raise Awareness

Raise awareness of its role as a primary Agency on the continent.

05

Research, Assessment and Information

Monitor, conduct research, assess and develop as well as disseminate regular and comprehensive data and information on humanitarian issues.

06

Policy and Strategic Advisory Role

The HHS has advisory role on policy and strategic issues, and it ensures synergy between the AfHA and the Commission.

05 | Operational Requirement

Humanitarian Access

To fulfill its mandate, the AfHA will need to have unimpeded access to humanitarian situations.

Source and institutional reference
The mandate, objectives and roles presented on this page are based on the relevant AfHA institutional and normative documentation. For authoritative legal and institutional formulations, users should consult the applicable AfHA Statute and other approved African Union documents.
