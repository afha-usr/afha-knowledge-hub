Mandate, Role & Objectives

Understanding the institutional purpose, responsibilities and strategic objectives of the African Humanitarian Agency.

Mandate Role Objectives Related Resources

The African Humanitarian Agency (AfHA) is established to strengthen Africa’s capacity to anticipate, prevent and respond to humanitarian crises while promoting a coordinated, effective and Africa-led humanitarian response.

01 | Mandate

A stronger Africa-led humanitarian response

AfHA provides an institutional framework for strengthening continental humanitarian preparedness, response and coordination.

AfHA's Mandate

AfHA's mandate is to strengthen Africa's capacity to anticipate, prevent and respond to humanitarian crises and to promote coordinated, effective and Africa-led humanitarian action.

Through this mandate, AfHA supports Member States and humanitarian actors in addressing the needs of populations affected by crises and emergencies across the continent.

AfHA contributes to a more coordinated and effective humanitarian system by supporting preparedness, response capacity, cooperation, knowledge and Africa-led humanitarian action.
02 | Role

Supporting Member States and humanitarian actors

AfHA's role is centred on strengthening capacity, coordination and cooperation across the African humanitarian landscape.

Support to Member States

Support Member States in strengthening their preparedness and capacity to anticipate, prevent and respond to humanitarian crises.

Humanitarian Coordination

Facilitate coordination and complementarity among relevant humanitarian actors and institutions involved in humanitarian action.

Preparedness and Response Capacity

Contribute to stronger institutional and operational capacity for humanitarian preparedness and emergency response.

Africa-led Humanitarian Action

Promote approaches that strengthen African ownership, leadership and participation in humanitarian action.

Knowledge and Learning

Support the sharing of knowledge, experience, good practices and lessons learned to strengthen humanitarian preparedness and response.

Cooperation and Partnerships

Strengthen cooperation and complementarity among African institutions, Member States and relevant humanitarian partners.

03 | Objectives

Key objectives

The objectives of AfHA are reflected in its focus on stronger preparedness, effective response, coordination, cooperation and Africa-led humanitarian action.

01
Strengthen preparedness Enhance the capacity of African institutions and Member States to prepare for humanitarian crises and emergencies.
02
Strengthen response capacity Support stronger and more effective humanitarian response capacities across the continent.
03
Promote coordination Encourage coordinated and complementary humanitarian action among relevant actors.
04
Advance Africa-led action Strengthen African leadership, ownership and participation in humanitarian response.
05
Promote cooperation Strengthen cooperation among Member States, African institutions and humanitarian actors.
06
Strengthen knowledge and learning Promote knowledge sharing, learning and the use of evidence to strengthen humanitarian action.
