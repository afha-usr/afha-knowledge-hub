AfHA Institutional Framework

Mandate, Role & Objectives

An overview of the purpose, role and key areas of action of the African Humanitarian Agency in strengthening Africa’s humanitarian capacity and promoting coordinated humanitarian action.

Overview Mandate Role Objectives Key Areas
01 | Overview

Strengthening Africa's humanitarian capacity

The African Humanitarian Agency (AfHA) is established to strengthen Africa’s capacity to anticipate, prevent and respond to humanitarian crises while promoting a coordinated, effective and Africa-led humanitarian response.

Its mandate and institutional purpose are situated within the broader efforts of the African Union to strengthen continental humanitarian action and support Member States and affected populations during crises and emergencies.

The Knowledge Hub provides access to key institutional and humanitarian reference materials supporting understanding of AfHA's mandate, role, principles and areas of work.

01

Africa-led humanitarian action

AfHA provides a continental framework for strengthening preparedness, response, coordination, capacity and humanitarian knowledge.

02 | Mandate

AfHA's Mandate

The mandate provides the foundation for AfHA's contribution to humanitarian preparedness and response across Africa.

CORE MANDATE

Strengthening humanitarian preparedness and response

AfHA is established to strengthen Africa’s capacity to anticipate, prevent and respond to humanitarian crises while promoting a coordinated, effective and Africa-led humanitarian response.

Reference: The authoritative formulation of AfHA's mandate, functions and institutional responsibilities should be read together with the AfHA Statute and other approved normative and institutional documents.
03 | Role

Role of AfHA

AfHA's role can be understood through the principal areas in which it contributes to stronger humanitarian action and institutional capacity.

01

Humanitarian Preparedness

Support efforts to strengthen preparedness, anticipation and readiness for humanitarian crises and emergencies.

02

Humanitarian Response

Contribute to effective and coordinated humanitarian response to crises and emergencies affecting populations across Africa.

03

Coordination

Strengthen coordination and complementarity among relevant African institutions, Member States and humanitarian actors.

04

Capacity Development

Support the development of humanitarian capacities, systems, knowledge and expertise across the continent.

05

Knowledge & Learning

Promote the generation, organization and sharing of humanitarian knowledge, learning and good practices.

06

African Ownership

Contribute to strengthening African leadership, ownership and participation in humanitarian action.

04 | Objectives

Key Objectives

The objectives below summarize the principal outcomes associated with AfHA's humanitarian purpose and institutional role.

01

Strengthen Preparedness

Strengthen continental and national capacity to anticipate and prepare for humanitarian crises and emergencies.

02

Improve Response Capacity

Support effective, timely and coordinated humanitarian response to affected populations.

03

Promote Coordination

Strengthen coordination, complementarity and collaboration among humanitarian actors and African institutions.

04

Strengthen African Capacity

Contribute to stronger African institutional, technical and operational capacity for humanitarian action.

05

Promote Knowledge

Strengthen humanitarian knowledge management, learning, information sharing and the use of evidence to support action.

06

Advance African Ownership

Support African leadership, ownership and participation in humanitarian preparedness and response.

05 | Key Areas

Areas of Humanitarian Action

The following areas provide a practical view of the humanitarian capacities and functions supported through AfHA's institutional purpose.

Preparedness & Anticipation

Strengthening preparedness, anticipation, prevention and readiness for humanitarian emergencies.

Emergency Response

Supporting coordinated humanitarian response and assistance to populations affected by crises and emergencies.

Coordination & Partnerships

Promoting coordination, complementarity and partnerships across relevant humanitarian institutions and actors.

Capacity Development

Supporting institutional, technical and operational capacities for humanitarian action.

Knowledge & Learning

Promoting knowledge management, learning, information sharing and good practices.

African Leadership

Supporting stronger African ownership, leadership and participation in humanitarian action.

Important: This page provides a concise Knowledge Hub presentation of AfHA's mandate, role and objectives. For authoritative legal and institutional wording, users should consult the AfHA Statute and other approved documents available through the Knowledge Resources section.
