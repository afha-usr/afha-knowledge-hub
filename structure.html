An overview of the institutional architecture through which the African Humanitarian Agency supports Africa-led humanitarian action.

The institutional structure of the African Humanitarian Agency provides a framework for leadership, governance, coordination and implementation of its humanitarian mandate.

02 | Governing Board

Composition of the Governing Board

The Governing Board is composed of fifteen (15) members, representing Member States, the African Union Commission, civil society and the private sector, and the United Nations humanitarian and human rights system.

15

Fifteen Members

The Board is answerable to the Chairperson of the AU Commission / STC and is composed of fifteen (15) members.

A

Ministers Responsible for Forced Displacement and Humanitarian Affairs

10 Members

Ten Ministers representing the five Regions of the African Union, with two Ministers per Region nominated by their respective Region.

North South Central East West
B

AU Commission Commissioners

2 Members · Non-voting Ex-officio

Two Commissioners of the AU Commission representing Political Affairs, Peace and Security and Social Development, Health and Humanitarian Affairs, serving as non-voting ex-officio members.

C

Private Sector and Civil Society

2 Members

Two nominees of the Chairperson of the Commission representing the Private Sector and Civil Society, in consultation with the Chairperson of the Board, including Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies.

D

United Nations Representative

1 Member · Rotational

One representative of UN Agencies with mandates on Human Rights and Humanitarian Issues, serving on a rotational basis.

Board Composition at a Glance

Governance note: The Board is answerable to the Chairperson of the AU Commission / STC. The two AU Commission Commissioners serve as non-voting ex-officio members, while the UN representative participates on a rotational basis.
