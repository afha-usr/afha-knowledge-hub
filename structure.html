03 | Governance

Governance and institutional relationships

AfHA's institutional arrangements are intended to support coordination, accountability, cooperation and effective delivery of its humanitarian mandate.

01
Strategic Direction Institutional governance provides direction for the Agency's overall humanitarian objectives and priorities.
02
Accountability Clear institutional responsibilities help support transparency and accountability.
03
Coordination Institutional relationships support coordination among relevant humanitarian actors.
04
Complementarity Cooperation and complementarity help connect continental, regional and national humanitarian capacities.
