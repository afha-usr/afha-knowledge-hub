An overview of the institutional architecture through which the African Humanitarian Agency supports Africa-led humanitarian action.

The institutional structure of the African Humanitarian Agency provides a framework for leadership, governance, coordination and implementation of its humanitarian mandate.

02 | Key Components

Institutional components

The principal levels shown in the institutional structure provide the framework for governance, strategic oversight, executive leadership and regional engagement.

01

African Union Commission

The AUC represents the continental institutional level within the structure shown above.

02

Specialized Technical Committee

The STC provides the relevant technical and policy-level institutional link within the structure.

03

AU-HHS

AU-HHS represents the African Union humanitarian and health-related institutional level shown in the AfHA hierarchy.

04

Governing Board

The Governing Board is represented in the structure as a 20-member governing body providing institutional oversight.

05

Executive Director

The Executive Director represents the executive leadership level responsible for leading the Agency's implementation and management.

06

Five Regions

AfHA's regional structure is represented through the North, South, Central, East and West regions.

03 | Governance

Governance and institutional relationships

The institutional structure establishes a clear relationship between continental oversight, technical and policy structures, governance, executive leadership and regional representation.

01
Continental Oversight The structure connects AfHA to the relevant African Union institutional architecture.
02
Governing Board The 20-member Governing Board forms the principal governance level shown in the institutional hierarchy.
03
Executive Leadership The Executive Director provides the executive leadership level below the Governing Board.
04
Regional Representation The five regional groupings provide the geographical dimension of AfHA's institutional structure.
