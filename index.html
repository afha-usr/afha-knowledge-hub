About AfHA

The African Humanitarian Agency (AfHA) is established to strengthen Africa's capacity to anticipate, prevent and respond to humanitarian crises while promoting a coordinated, effective and Africa-led humanitarian response.

Through its mandate, AfHA works to support Member States and humanitarian actors in addressing the needs of populations affected by crises and emergencies across the continent.

The AfHA is established as a Specialized Technical Institution of the African Union charged with the responsibility to coordinate and provide humanitarian responses in coordination with affected Member States.

AfHA at a Glance

Mandate, objectives and roles of the African Humanitarian Agency.

The African Humanitarian Agency (AfHA) is established to strengthen Africa's capacity to anticipate, prevent and respond to humanitarian crises while promoting a coordinated, effective and Africa-led humanitarian response.

Through its mandate, AfHA works to support Member States and humanitarian actors in addressing the needs of populations affected by crises and emergencies across the continent.

The AfHA is hereby established as a Specialized Technical Institution of the African Union charged with the responsibility to coordinate and provide humanitarian responses in coordination with affected Member States.

01

Mandates

  • Streamline the coordination of humanitarian action and response on the continent with Member States.
  • Provide assistance and ensure effective coordination of humanitarian action on the continent.
  • Build and strengthen the capabilities and capacities of AU Member States, Regional Bodies and Mechanisms in preventing, responding, effectively managing, alleviating and contributing to resolving the humanitarian crises on the continent.
02

Objectives

  • Prevent humanitarian crises from unfolding through improved early warning and early action, including anticipatory action, thereby ensuring timely responses to situations that may otherwise develop into humanitarian crises.
  • Enhance the capacity of AU Member States, RECs and AU bodies in cooperation with other agencies to prevent and respond to humanitarian crises.
  • Ensure timely and effective responses through establishing and strengthening capacities of continental, regional and national mechanisms.
  • Together with Member States, design strategies for strengthening adaptation and resilience at the continental, regional and national levels in order to ensure an effective response to humanitarian crises.
03

Roles

  • Identify and map humanitarian crises on the continent.
  • Provide humanitarian assistance to AU Member States and persons affected by humanitarian crises. To this end, the AfHA will establish five regional centres to ensure proximity for an effective and timely humanitarian response in each region. These regional centres will also include regional depots to stockpile humanitarian assistance.
  • Provide strong coordination mechanisms on humanitarian issues, including with the African Standby Force (the Civil military element).
  • Raise awareness of its role as a primary Agency on the continent.
  • Monitor, conduct research, assess and develop as well as disseminate regular and comprehensive data and information on humanitarian issues.
  • The HHS has advisory role on policy and strategic issues, and it ensures synergy between the AfHA and the Commission.
  • To fulfill its mandate, the AfHA will need to have unimpeded access to humanitarian situations.
