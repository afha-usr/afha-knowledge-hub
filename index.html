About AfHA

The African Humanitarian Agency (AfHA) is an African Union humanitarian mechanism established to strengthen continental humanitarian action and support more effective responses to humanitarian crises across Africa.


The AfHA Knowledge Hub brings together key humanitarian frameworks, policies, guidance, tools, reports and learning resources in one accessible platform.

Guiding Principles

The humanitarian action of AfHA is guided by fundamental principles that support effective, accountable and inclusive humanitarian action.

Leadership Strong continental humanitarian leadership.
Humanity Respect for human dignity and human life.
Impartiality Assistance based on humanitarian need.
Independence Independent humanitarian action.
Neutrality Humanitarian action without taking sides.
Solidarity Collective African humanitarian responsibility.
Accountability Transparent and responsible humanitarian action.
Participation & Ownership Meaningful participation of affected communities.
Subsidiarity & Complementarity Action at the appropriate level and coordination.
Gender Mainstreaming Integrating gender considerations across action.
Vulnerable Groups Attention to people with special needs.
