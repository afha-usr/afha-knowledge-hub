About AfHA

Understanding the African Humanitarian Agency and its role within the African humanitarian architecture.

The African Humanitarian Agency (AfHA) is established as a specialized technical institution of the Commission charged with the responsibility to coordinate humanitarian responses in Member States.

This knowledge hub provides an overview of AfHA's background, mandate, roles and objectives, institutional structure, normative frameworks and operationalization.

It also provides access to humanitarian knowledge resources intended to support coordination, preparedness, response, learning and evidence-based humanitarian action across the continent.

A Continental Knowledge Resource

The platform brings together key humanitarian reference materials in one accessible location, supporting practitioners, institutions, Member States and partners engaged in humanitarian action in Africa.

AfHA at a Glance

Key elements of the African Humanitarian Agency's institutional mandate and role.

01

Mandate

AfHA is mandated to support the coordination and strengthening of humanitarian action across African Union Member States.

02

Role

To contribute to a coordinated, effective and African-led humanitarian response architecture.

03

Objectives

Strengthening preparedness, coordination, response capacity, knowledge and humanitarian leadership.

04

Structure

An institutional structure designed to support continental and regional humanitarian action.

Guiding Principles

Core principles guiding humanitarian action and the work of the African Humanitarian Agency.

01

Leadership

Promoting African leadership and ownership of humanitarian action.

02

Humanity

Addressing human suffering wherever it is found, with particular attention to those most affected.

03

Impartiality

Providing assistance based on needs without discrimination.

04

Independence

Maintaining operational independence in humanitarian decision-making and action.

05

Neutrality

Maintaining a position that supports humanitarian action without taking sides in conflicts.

06

Solidarity

Strengthening collective responsibility and solidarity in addressing humanitarian challenges.

07

Accountability

Promoting accountability to affected people, institutions and partners.

08

Participation & Ownership

Ensuring affected populations and relevant stakeholders participate in humanitarian action.

09

Subsidiarity & Complementarity

Supporting action at the most appropriate level while complementing existing humanitarian mechanisms.

10

Gender Mainstreaming

Integrating gender considerations across humanitarian action and decision-making.

11

Vulnerable Groups

Ensuring attention to vulnerable groups and people with special needs.
